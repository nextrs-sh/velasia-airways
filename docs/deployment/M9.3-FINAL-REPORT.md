## 6. Clock and TTL correctness

Implemented; `m9-3-clock.test.ts` (9 tests) covers deterministic fixtures under a pinned date,
the 60 s reuse window (+59 s reused, +61 s new), 15-minute validity, no reuse after expiry,
hold expiry, restart behaviour (sessions compared in real time across restarts, offers never
carried over) and the created-offers counter. Unpinned behaviour is unchanged by construction
(both clocks equal) and the whole existing suite passes. Two crash tests that only worked
because of the frozen clock now age stored expiries instead.
[Details](M9.3-BENCHMARK-CORRECTNESS.md).
